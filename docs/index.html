Privacy Policy

App: War Room (Android)
Last updated: November 15, 2026

War Room is a collection of ten single-player strategy games set in historical and recent conflicts. This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect.

Information we collect

The app does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. The games run entirely on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your gameplay, and we do not store your game progress.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use certain data from your device to show and measure ads, including your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Google may use this data to show personalized or non-personalized ads, depending on your choices and where you live.

You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Consent: In regions where the law requires it (for example the EEA and the UK), the app shows Google's consent form before ads are requested, so you can choose how your data is used for ads.
  • Advertising ID: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Children

War Room is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party involved is Google, through AdMob, as described above. Because the app does not collect personal data itself, we do not hold any data about you that could be exposed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
